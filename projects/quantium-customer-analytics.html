Quantium Customer Analytics

Chip-category transaction and customer data, cleaned and analyzed in Python to find what drives sales.

Business problem

A chips retailer saw sales decline and wanted to understand why, with insight into customer purchasing behaviour and product performance.

Approach

  • Python for data preparation (ETL) and exploratory analysis of the transaction and customer files.
  • Time-series view of sales trends.
  • Customer segmentation by life stage, purchase frequency and order value.
  • Store-level comparison of purchases.

Key findings

  • 175g packs are the best-selling size, followed by 150g; 125g and 180g packs sell the least.
  • New Families and Midage Singles/Couples are the life stages that buy the least.
  • Units sold dropped in August–September 2018 and in May 2019, pulling down total revenue.
  • Stores 211, 76, 11, 252, 206, 92, 193, 85 and 31 have the lowest purchases.

Recommendations

  • Target New Families and Midage Singles/Couples.
  • Stronger offers on 125g, 180g and 70g packs; competitive promotions on 175g and 150g.
  • Review the sales plan for the lowest-purchasing stores.
  • Targeted win-back campaigns with personalised discounts for lapsed customers.
