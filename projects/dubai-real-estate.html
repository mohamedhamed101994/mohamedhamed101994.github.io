Dubai Real Estate Market (2000–2026)

26 years of official Dubai property transactions — how the market moves, and where activity and value concentrate.

Business problem

What do 26 years of official transaction data show about how the Dubai property market moves, and where activity and value are concentrated?

Data & preparation

  • Official Dubai Land Department transaction data, published on the Dubai Data open-data platform.
  • The raw dataset starts in 1963 and has data-quality issues, so it was cleaned in Python (Pandas) first.
  • Earlier years are incomplete, so the analysis is scoped to 2000–2026: 874K+ transactions worth AED 3.4T, across 243 areas and 3,266 projects.

Key findings

64% / 36%Existing vs. Off-Plan share of transactions
69K · AED 208bnMarsa Dubai — #1 area by count and value
−27.9%Transaction value in 2009, after the 2008 crisis
  • Sales are about 77% of transactions, mortgages about 20% and gifts about 4%.
  • Flats are the most traded type (559K transactions), but land has the highest total value (about AED 1.16T).

Approach

  • Four report pages: Overview, Market Trend, Areas and Projects.
  • Transaction count and value over time, year-over-year growth, price per sq m trends, area rankings, property types and usage, and registration type (Off-Plan vs. Existing).
  • Slicers by area, property type, nearest metro, mall and landmark.

2026 is a partial year. Average price per sq m is sensitive to outliers in low-volume areas.
