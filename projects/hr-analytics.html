HR Analytics Dashboard

An interactive HR dashboard covering hiring, turnover, demographics, leave patterns and work location across six departments.

Data

A public Kaggle HR dataset with 1,000 hiring records and 750 current employees across six departments.

What the dashboard covers

  • Headcount: total headcount, active vs. resigned, and turnover rate.
  • Hiring: hiring trends 2015–2024 and hiring sources (LinkedIn, walk-in, agency, website, referral), including resignations by source.
  • Demographics: age and gender, plus headcount by department and job title.
  • Leave: sick-leave and days-off patterns by department.
  • Work location: headquarters vs. work-from-home split.
  • Navigation: bookmarks and custom tooltips.
