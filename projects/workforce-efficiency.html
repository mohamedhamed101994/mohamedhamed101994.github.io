Workforce Efficiency (What-If)Full marks

Are project teams staffed well enough to deliver on time — and what happens to delivery if capacity changes?

Business problem

Do project teams have enough people to finish on schedule, and how does delivery change if working hours or headcount change?

Data

A challenge dataset of 296 employees, 20 projects, 630 work shifts and about 2K tasks across Egypt, UAE, KSA and Qatar.

Key findings

40%Headcount below project requirements
50%Average employee utilization
34%Turnover
25%Tasks closed

Approach

  • Overview: attendance and key summary metrics.
  • Employees: utilization and turnover by department.
  • Projects: headcount gap against requirements, task status and project duration by location.
  • What-If Scenarios: parameters for working hours and employee count show the effect on project duration and flag projects at risk of delay.
